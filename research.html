Research

A few projects are in progress below. Some earlier work is temporarily offline — I'm always glad to discuss it, so please get in touch.

Gender and Legislative Politics in JordanIn progress

With Marwa Shalaby. How gender shapes legislative behavior and representation in Jordan's parliament.

Comparative politics · Gender & representation · Jordan

Framing Women's Reform in the GulfIn progress

With Aili Tripp. A discourse analysis of how Gulf states frame women's-rights reform in official texts and state media.

Policy discourse analysis · Gender · Gulf states

When Signals Collide: How Domestic Publics Interpret Conflicting SignalsIn progress

A survey experiment on how publics weigh contradictory signals from a foreign country's government and its society.

International relations · Survey experiment · Political behavior

Ethnic Heterogeneity in SarajevoIn progress

With Marko Kljajić and Nadav Shelef. Measuring fine-grained ethnic heterogeneity in Sarajevo and its political consequences.

Ethnic politics · Spatial data · Bosnia

Religious Doctrine vs. Institutions in Political SpeechIn progress

Uses natural language processing to distinguish whether religious references in political speech emphasize group identity or personal religiosity. The pipeline fine-tunes sentence-BERT classifiers, applies SHAP-based interpretability to understand model behavior, and traces how religious framing shifts over time.

Natural language processing · Sentence-BERT · SHAP · Political discourse

State Reactions to Protest MovementsIn progress

A comparative study of how regimes across the Middle East and North Africa respond to mass mobilization. Drawing on country- and event-level data, the project classifies regime types and analyzes the short-term strategies states use to contain, co-opt, or repress dissent.

Comparative politics · Event data · Regime analysis · MENA
