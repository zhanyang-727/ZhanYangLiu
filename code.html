Religious Framing ClassifierIn development

A natural language processing pipeline that classifies religious references in political speech as emphasizing group identity or personal religiosity, using a fine-tuned sentence-BERT model with SHAP interpretability — from annotation and preprocessing through training and analysis.

Python · Transformers · Sentence-BERT · SHAP

Political Opinion Clustering in the Middle EastResearch

Applies unsupervised learning (PCA, K-means) to election survey data to surface distinct clusters of political attitudes among voters in the Middle East, with clean visual summaries of each latent group and its demographic correlates.

Python · PCA · K-means · Survey data
